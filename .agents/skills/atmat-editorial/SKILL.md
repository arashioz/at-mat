# AtMat editorial workflow

Ground weight, ingredients, storage, and the 20-minute boil in the brine label. Drained weight 72% is not protein. Do not add health, protein, sustainability, or «no oil» claims. Omit nutrition numbers you did not re-read on the label this session.

Serving ideas (salad, sandwich, pasta) stay honest: they are not tested or published recipes. The ماهی / آب / نمک beats use only label facts. Creative lines stay separate from the boil warning, and the warning is not hidden in motion.
