# AtMat brand direction

## Establish the source

The repo `PHASES.md` is the ERP plan. The brine consumer page in `web/` is an explicit exception. Read the approved brine label before any claim. Strategy text is not proof of ingredients, nutrition, or method.

Voice is bold, playful, energetic Persian. Latin type is graphic seasoning only (`IN BRINE`). Essential facts stay Persian.

## Apply the direction

- Family signature is the logo green and yellow, used once. Do not repeat a yellow bar.
- The brine page world is the packaging blue, sampled from the real can. Label samples as working colors, not official brand HEX.
- No sea photography on the brine page. The hero is the real can.
- Keep the label artwork intact. Do not redraw or rewrite it.
- The brine page includes a three-beat deconstruction: ماهی / آب / نمک, using only label facts.
- Upload images through `POST /api/storage/images`. Use returned URLs only.

## Done checks

Flavor color owns the page, the can is the hero, claims match the label, and green/yellow is a signature rather than a stripe.
